Privacy Policy

Jessica LifeOS · 生效日 / Effective 2026-10-02

1. 這個應用程式是什麼What this application is

Jessica LifeOS 是 Jessica Hsia 為自己打造的個人自動化工具，僅供本人使用。 它沒有對外開放註冊，沒有其他使用者，也不是商業服務。

Jessica LifeOS is a personal automation tool that Jessica Hsia built for herself and uses only for her own account. It has no public sign-up, no other users, and is not a commercial service.

2. 我們讀取哪些資料What data is accessed

此應用只讀取擁有者本人的以下 Google 資料：

  • Google Calendar：活動標題與時間，用於列出當天與隔天的行程。
    Google Calendar — event titles and times, used to list today's and tomorrow's schedule.
  • Gmail：未讀郵件的寄件者與主旨。
    Gmail — sender and subject lines of unread messages.

此應用不會讀取郵件內文、附件，也不會修改、寄送或刪除任何郵件或行事曆活動。

The application does not read message bodies or attachments, and does not modify, send, or delete any email or calendar event.

3. 資料用途How the data is used

上述資料只有一個用途：產生一份給擁有者本人看的個人日報，摘要當天的天氣、行程、 郵件與待辦事項。

The data has exactly one purpose: generating a personal daily report for the owner, summarising the day's weather, schedule, email, and tasks.

資料不會用於廣告、分析、訓練模型，或任何其他目的。

The data is not used for advertising, analytics, model training, or any other purpose.

4. 資料儲存位置Where data is stored

所有資料只存在擁有者本人的電腦上，以及擁有者自己的私人筆記空間。 沒有對外伺服器，沒有資料庫，沒有雲端備份給第三方。

All data stays on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own private notes. There is no external server, no database, and no third-party cloud backup.

5. 資料分享Data sharing

不分享。不販售。資料不會傳送、揭露、出租或出售給任何第三方、廣告商或資料仲介。

No sharing. No selling. Data is never transmitted, disclosed, rented, or sold to any third party, advertiser, or data broker.

6. 如何撤銷授權How to revoke access

擁有者可隨時在 Google 帳戶設定中撤銷此應用程式的授權，撤銷後此應用立即無法再讀取任何資料：

The owner can revoke this application's access at any time in Google Account settings. Once revoked, the application can no longer read any data:

myaccount.google.com/permissions

7. 資料保留與刪除Retention and deletion

每天的日報會覆寫或存檔於擁有者自己的筆記中。擁有者可隨時手動刪除本機檔案與筆記頁面， 即為完整刪除。

Each day's report is written into the owner's own notes, where it may be archived or overwritten. The owner can delete the local files and note pages at any time, which constitutes complete deletion.

若此政策有變更，本頁面會更新並修改生效日期。有任何疑問請聯絡擁有者 Jessica Hsia： jessica4188323@gmail.com

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with its effective date. For questions, contact the owner, Jessica Hsia, at jessica4188323@gmail.com.
